Q3. Outcome-knowability:

    The schema establishes the ISSUE date and a CONTRACTED end date (issue + duration). It does NOT record the actual completion date or the snapshot extraction date. Groups A/B are terminal snapshots; C/D are censored. A prior loan's final outcome can be treated as 'knowable at the later issue date' only by ASSUMING the loan matured on its contracted schedule and that no C/D loan is used. Counts of outcome-knowable pairs above include that assumption.

Q4. Later loans eligible for a leakage-safe prior feature:

DECISIVE CONCLUSION:
    ZERO clients are associated with more than one loan account in the PKDD '99 snapshot. Every loan-bearing account is owned by a distinct, unique client, and even including DISPONENT links every client still maps to exactly one loan account (827 distinct clients, all with one loan link each). Consequently there are ZERO earlier->later same-client loan pairs to reconstruct, so a previous-loan repayment feature cannot be built from this dataset at all - independent of the outcome-knowability question.
