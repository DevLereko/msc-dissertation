## Temporal split (Section 4.5.1) — data/processed/split_meta.json
- Train: 409 loans (1993-07-05 to 1997-06-13); targets 354/55
- Val:   136 loans (1997-06-13 to 1998-01-27); targets 118/18
- Test:  137 loans (1998-01-27 to 1998-12-08); targets 134/3

NOTE: The purely chronological split yields only 3 default-class cases in the
test set. Ranking metrics (ROC-AUC, PR-AUC) remain meaningful, but test
recall/precision are noisy. This is a genuine finding for Chapter 5.
